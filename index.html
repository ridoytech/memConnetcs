Why was MEM Connects started?

Our Story: From One Student’s Struggle to Hundreds of Successes

2018

The Problem

In 2018, our founder wanted to go to the UK as a student himself. However, he got stuck facing three major problems: a visa rejection, selecting the wrong university, and failing to find accommodation after reaching London. After much hardship and trial and error, when he finally made it to the UK, he realized—"This journey shouldn't have been this difficult."

2020

The Solution

That is exactly when he decided—having gone through this difficult journey himself, he would ensure that other students wouldn't have to endure the same hardships. And that is how MEM Connects started in 2020—a platform where students receive not just application guidance, but support for the entire journey: from career counseling to setting up bank accounts, finding housing, and securing part-time jobs upon arriving in the UK—absolutely everything.

Today

The Result

Today, 500+ Bangladeshi students have reached the UK with us. A 95%+ visa success rate. 20+ university partners. But our biggest success? When a student says—"I couldn't have made it here without you."
